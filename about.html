Behind the Case Files

About Dugout Detectives

Dugout Detectives is a softball mystery puzzle series that challenges readers to follow the evidence, eliminate thousands of names, and uncover the one player who belongs at the center of the case.

Official Case File Headquarters

The Idea

A Mystery Built for Softball Fans

Dugout Detectives was created to combine the energy of the softball field with the satisfaction of solving a carefully constructed logic mystery.

Each case begins with a massive roster of possible names. Readers study the evidence, apply every clue, and eliminate players one group at a time until only one final name remains.

The goal is not simply to finish a puzzle. It is to make readers feel like they have stepped into an active investigation and solved the case themselves.

The Creator

Meet AJ Freeman

AJ Freeman is the creator of the Dugout Detectives series.

The series was developed around a simple question:

What if a softball puzzle book felt like opening a real detective case file?

That idea became a mystery experience built around evidence files, player rosters, logic, deduction, and one missing Championship MVP.

Made Possible by a Community

More Than 100,000 Shared Names

Dugout Detectives would not exist without the 100,000+ people who believed in the idea and shared their first names for Puzzle Book 1.

Those names helped create the player database that became the foundation of Case File #001: The Missing MVP.

To everyone who contributed, encouraged the project, shared the idea, or followed the investigation: thank you for helping bring Dugout Detectives to life.
